03

Security and isolation

RBAC

Different permissions for registration, validation, structure and sensitive operations.

Multi-tenant RLS

PostgreSQL enforces organization isolation and tests validate tenant separation.

CAS and protected RPCs

Critical operations validate tenant, role and version to reduce silent overwrites.

Secrets outside the frontend

Sensitive credentials stay outside the client; integrations are prepared for backend/Edge Functions.

04

P9 — Explainable Asset Intelligence

Evidence-based priority

Deterministic ranking combines severity and confidence and shows the signals behind each priority.

Operational dashboard

Filters, pagination and CSV export for analysis of priorities and asset signals.

Economic profile

Acquisition, value, currency, useful life, source and reference remain separate from the official inventory contract.

Warranties and coverage

Expired or near-expiration coverage generates signals; missing data is reported as insufficient.

Recurring maintenance

Signals only exist when enough real operational history is available.

Explicit uncertainty

The platform prefers to declare data gaps rather than invent age, TCO, warranty or maintenance assumptions.
